Welcome

Welcome to the Donation Transparency page of Duminda Donation Website.

My name is Duminda, and I sincerely appreciate every person who visits this website and considers supporting my journey through voluntary donations.

I believe that trust is one of the most important parts of receiving financial support. Every supporter deserves to understand why donations are requested, how contributions are intended to be used, and how important their generosity is.

My commitment is to provide clear, honest, and understandable information about the purpose of this fundraising website.

1. Why We Receive Donations

The purpose of this website is to receive voluntary financial contributions from generous individuals who wish to support my personal journey, financial needs, and future goals.

Donations are intended to help me manage financial challenges, cover essential expenses, and continue working towards personal and online development projects.

Every donation is appreciated, regardless of its amount.

2. How Donations Will Be Used

Contributions received through this website may be allocated towards the following purposes:

  • Personal and Essential Expenses - Financial assistance for necessary personal expenses and everyday living costs.
  • Financial Support - Assistance with unexpected expenses and other genuine financial difficulties.
  • Website Development - Costs associated with creating, improving, hosting, and maintaining websites.
  • Digital Projects and Creativity - Expenses related to digital creativity, AI art projects, online activities, and creative development.
  • Technology and Learning - Digital tools, software, educational resources, and opportunities to develop new skills.
  • Future Development - Financial resources to help achieve personal goals and explore future opportunities.

The actual allocation of donations will depend on the amount received and the financial priorities at the time.

3. Our Payment Methods

For convenience, supporters can use the following payment methods:

  • PayPal - Donation email: dkoswatta9@gmail.com
  • Payoneer - Payment contact email: dkoswatta9@gmail.com
  • Bank Transfer - Supporters can use the bank account details published on our Donate Now page.

All payments should be made through the appropriate official payment channels.

4. Responsible Management of Donations

I understand that every contribution represents someone's kindness and trust.

I aim to manage donations responsibly and use contributions according to the purposes explained on this website.

I will make reasonable efforts to:

  • Use donations for their stated fundraising purposes.
  • Keep accurate personal records of donations received and expenses.
  • Avoid misleading statements about fundraising activities.
  • Provide clear information about fundraising goals.
  • Communicate honestly about how financial support is intended to help.

5. Donation Records and Updates

I value transparency and want supporters to understand the progress of my fundraising activities.

Where practical, I may provide updates about:

  • Fundraising progress.
  • General amounts received.
  • Important expenses supported by donations.
  • Website and creative project development.
  • Progress towards personal goals.

For privacy and security reasons, sensitive financial information, account numbers, and personal payment details will not be published.

6. Voluntary Donations

All contributions made through this website are voluntary.

There is no obligation to donate, and visitors should only contribute an amount they can comfortably afford.

Your personal financial responsibilities should always come first.

If you are unable to donate, sharing this website or offering words of encouragement is also appreciated.

7. Payment Security

Your payment security is important.

Donations should be completed through official payment services and verified bank transfer information.

This website will never ask visitors to provide their PayPal passwords, Payoneer passwords, banking passwords, card PINs, or other confidential login information.

Please verify the recipient and payment details before confirming any transaction.

8. Our Commitment to You

I sincerely respect every supporter who chooses to contribute.

My commitment is to:

  • Be honest about the purpose of this website.
  • Explain the intended use of donations.
  • Appreciate every contribution.
  • Avoid making unrealistic promises.
  • Provide updates when possible.
  • Respect the privacy of supporters.

Your trust and generosity are extremely valuable to me.

9. Thank You for Your Trust and Generosity

Every donation represents an act of kindness and support.

I understand that people work hard for their money, and I never take your generosity for granted.

Thank you for believing in my journey, supporting my goals, and helping me work towards a brighter future.

With sincere gratitude,

Duminda
